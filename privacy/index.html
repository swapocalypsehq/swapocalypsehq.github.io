Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Swapocalypse is a small clay stop-motion series on TikTok and Instagram. This page covers our website and our private analytics tool, Swapocalypse Stats.

Suggest a Swap form

When you suggest a swap, we receive the swap you typed and, if you give one, your social handle. The form is delivered to us by email through FormSubmit. We use it only to pick ideas for episodes and to credit you. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else, and we don't add you to any list.

Swapocalypse Stats

Swapocalypse Stats is an internal tool that only our own team uses. With our own permission, it reads statistics for our own Swapocalypse accounts through official platform APIs (for example TikTok's follower, like and video view counts). It does not read anyone else's account, messages or personal information, and it never posts or acts on any account.

The numbers are stored on our own computer and in a private dashboard that only we can open. Access keys are kept in the computer's secure keychain. We don't sell or share this data.

Removing your information

To have a suggestion or credit removed, message @swapocalypse on TikTok or Instagram and we'll delete it. Our access to our own accounts can be revoked at any time in each platform's settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
